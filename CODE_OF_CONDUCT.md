# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in this project a respectful and welcoming experience for everyone.

## Our Standards
Examples of positive behavior include:
- Being respectful and considerate.
- Accepting constructive feedback.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.
- Showing kindness toward other contributors.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, insulting comments, and personal attacks.

## Enforcement Responsibilities
Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards.

## Scope
This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces and public interactions related to this project.

## Enforcement
Maintainers may review reported incidents and take appropriate action to protect the community.

## Enforcement Guidelines
Maintainers will consider the nature and impact of each incident and respond fairly and consistently.

## Attribution
This Code of Conduct was prepared using common open-source community guidelines as a reference.
